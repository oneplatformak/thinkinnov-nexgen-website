01Overview

What it is

Lifecycle management for product data, collaboration and change control.

Siemens describes Teamcenter as product lifecycle management (PLM) software that connects people and processes across the product lifecycle. Source (opens in a new tab)

Siemens lists this name among the trademarks or registered trademarks of Siemens Industry Software Inc., or its subsidiaries or affiliates. Source (opens in a new tab)

This page describes how ThinkInnov-NexGen works with the product. For features, versions and licensing, please refer to Siemens.
