Driving Digital

Digital Transformation

ThinkInnov-NexGen enables digital transformation with Product Data Maturity Model strategy, roadmap thinking and connected product data platforms.

01PDMM

Product Data Maturity Model

PDMM defines the maturity of product data managed by a company and helps create the PLM strategy and roadmap.

  1. PDMM L1

    System Definition

    Defines the data and information that must be collected or managed for the product.

  2. PDMM L2

    System to Digital

    Structured and connected data management with a digital foundation.

  3. PDMM L3

    Augmented Intelligence Platform

    Effective usage of existing product data to reduce time-to-market and cost.

  4. PDMM L4

    Innovation Platform

    Engineering-powered predictive layer for the right feature mix of the product.
