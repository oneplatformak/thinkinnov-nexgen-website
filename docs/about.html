Who We Are

ThinkInnov-NexGen is a next-generation IT services company helping businesses solve complex problems with modern cloud, IoT, AI and digital talent. The organization focuses on accelerating digital transformation with practical, human-centered technology delivery.

What is ThinkInnov-NexGen?

A global software development services provider serving customers since 2014.

Operating Philosophy

Create meaningful solutions from inspiration, with disciplined engineering and a customer-first delivery model.

Global Presence

Japan headquarters with India delivery presence across Chennai and Coimbatore.

Vision, Mission and Values

Vision

To become a pioneer in next-generation IT services and solutions, empowering individuals and organizations to lead change.

Mission

To deliver effective IT services and solutions based on professional experience, best practices, global expertise and advanced technologies.

Values

Head, Heart and Hand: wisdom, passion and execution. ThinkInnov-NexGen believes in giving back to customers, teams and society.
