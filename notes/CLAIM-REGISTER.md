## 2. Neutral explanatory text written for this build

The lifecycle diagram needs one sentence per stage. Staging names the lifecycle but does not describe the stages, so these nine sentences are new. They describe what generally happens at a stage of any product lifecycle. They say nothing about ThinkInnov-NexGen, its customers or any Siemens product.

| Stage | Sentence |
| --- | --- |
| Idea | Ideas and market needs are captured and assessed so that the strongest candidates move forward as product decisions. |
| Requirements | Customer, internal and regulatory needs are written down as requirements that the product must satisfy and can later be verified against. |
| Design | Mechanical and electrical teams define the product in 3D models and schematics, reusing existing parts and knowledge where they can. |
| Engineering | Design data is released, revised and configured under change control so that every team works from the same product definition. |
| Validation | Simulation and testing check the design against its requirements before it is committed to production. |
| Manufacturing | Production processes are planned, and engineering data is handed over to the ERP and MES systems that run the factory. |
| Quality | Quality is planned alongside the design, followed through production, and issues are traced back to their cause. |
| Service | Products in use generate real-world feedback that shows how they actually perform. |
| Continuous improvement | Feedback and lessons learned return to design as changes, so the next revision starts from what is already known. |
