# Japanese translation register

Generated by `node improved-src/build.js`. Do not edit by hand: change the dictionaries in `improved-src/i18n/` and rebuild.

Every Japanese string on the `/ja/` pages comes from one of two places:

- **Staging** (141 strings in use, plus the 4 testimonial renderings): the Japanese text appears verbatim in the staging mirror (`staging-original/`). It was written or approved by whoever produced the staging site and is reused unchanged.
- **New** (748 strings and 6 sentence patterns): written for this build by an AI system, not by a native-speaking professional translator. **None of it has had native review.** It should be read by a native Japanese speaker with PLM knowledge before launch.

The build does not hide gaps: it reports every English string on a Japanese page that has no entry. The current count is in the build output and in `notes/REVIEW.md`.

## Decisions a reviewer should check first

- **About Us** is 会社情報 and **Company Profile** is 会社概要. Staging used 会社概要 for both; they are different sections of the same page here, so the page name was changed. This is the only place where staging Japanese was overridden.
- **Siemens product names** (NX, Solid Edge, Capital, Teamcenter, Tecnomatix, Mendix) and the company name stay in Latin script.
- **Testimonials** show the original English as the authoritative text. The Japanese beneath each is the staging rendering, labelled 参考訳 (reference translation).
- **People's names and customer names** are not transliterated.
- **Job titles and job requirements** are translated; staging left them in English. Indian salary terms (CTC, LPA) are kept as abbreviations.
- **Register**: です・ます throughout, with polite request forms in forms and calls to action.
- **The privacy notice** (個人情報の取り扱い) also needs legal review; the Japanese is a translation of the English notice and has no independent legal standing.

## New sentence patterns

These are built from parts by rule rather than stored as whole sentences:

- Photo viewer labels: “Enlarge photo N of 14” → 写真を拡大（14枚中N枚目）
- Infographic labels: “Infographic: <industry>” → インフォグラフィック：<industry>
- Experience: “2+ Years” → 2年以上; “4 - 6 Years” → 4〜6年
- “Other roles in <discipline>” → 「<discipline>」のその他の職種
- “Careers that mention <product>” → <product>に関連する募集職種
- Job page descriptions: ThinkInnov-NexGenの募集職種：<title>（<location>、<experience>）。<summary>
- Page titles are translated part by part around the “|” separator.
